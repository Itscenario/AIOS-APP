## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional aesthetic tailored for a premier national medical association. The visual direction balances academic prestige with clinical precision, projecting unquestioned trust, regulatory rigor, and digital efficiency for healthcare professionals and ophthalmologists.

The design movement combines **Corporate / Modern** structure with **Clinical Minimalism**:
- **Dignified Institutional Authority**: Deep nautical navies and restrained warm gold accents evoke tradition, board certification, and academic excellence without feeling dated.
- **Sterile Clarity & Legibility**: Form fields, verification prompts, and authentication states prioritize zero-error data capture, high-contrast feedback, and reduced visual fatigue under hospital lighting conditions.
- **Tactile Subtlety**: Flat clinical planes are grounded by ultra-fine hairline structural strokes and softly diffused, cool-tinted elevation layers that maintain crisp boundaries across desktop stations and mobile clinical devices.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for clinical authority, institutional weight, and unambiguous visual state differentiation.

### Color Roles & Meaning
- **Primary Navy (`#0F294A`, `#1A365D`)**: Anchors primary navigation, association branding, institutional crest frames, and dominant CTA buttons. Signifies official verification and governance.
- **Secondary Medical Royal (`#1E40AF`, `#2563EB`)**: Serves as the functional interactive layer for links, input focus halos, active step indicators, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary Saffron Gold (`#D97706`, `#B45309`)**: Reserved as an honorific accent—used sparingly for verified society member badges, fellowship credentials, and priority verification ribbons.
- **Clinical Neutrals & Canvas**:
  - `Surface Base`: `#F8FAFC` (clinical cool slate ground).
  - `Card / Container Surface`: `#FFFFFF` (crisp optical white).
  - `Border Neutral`: `#E2E8F0` (subtle structural divider) and `#CBD5E1` (unfocused field borders).
  - `Text Primary`: `#0F172A` (deep ink black for peak readability).
  - `Text Muted`: `#64748B` (slate gray for secondary labels, hints, and timestamps).
- **Clinical Feedback**:
  - `Success`: `#059669` (emerald green for verified credentials, matched licenses, OTP match).
  - `Error`: `#DC2626` (crimson for failed verification, expired registration, invalid inputs).
  - `Warning`: `#D97706` (amber for pending review and renewal warnings).

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the structural warmth and sculpted geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles, headings, and numerical codes with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter** for forms, table data, legal disclaimers, and micro-labels.

### Typographic Principles
- **Clarity Over Embellishment**: Medical council numbers, National Medical Commission (NMC) codes, and OTP digits require immediate character distinction (e.g., distinguishing `0` from `O`, `1` from `l`).
- **Hierarchy Separation**: High-density medical forms use `label-md` in medium weight (500) paired with uppercase `label-sm` tracking (+0.04em) for auxiliary metadata, credential tags, and verification statuses.
- **OTP Scale**: The dedicated `code-otp` token ensures numeric credentials stand out prominently during multi-factor authentication sequences on mobile and desktop viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

Authentication interfaces demand focused cognitive load reduction. The layout philosophy relies on a **Centric Constrained Grid** on desktop and a **Single-Column Fluid Shell** on mobile.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: Authentication containers use a centered structural card with a maximum width of `480px` for standard login/OTP and `640px` for detailed multi-step registration (incorporating medical license uploads). For dual-column splash layouts, a 12-column grid splits into a 5-column institutional brand panel (Navy background with society seal) and a 7-column active authentication canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Fixed centered card with `margin: 2rem` side gutters.
- **Mobile (under 768px)**: Fluid full-width container running with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` outer padding, pinned bottom CTAs on mobile keyboards where appropriate.

### Rhythmic Scale
Spacing strictly follows an 8pt architectural rhythm, with 4pt (`space-xs`) reserved for tight input-to-label vertical coupling and badge padding. Vertical margins between form input blocks default consistently to `space-lg` (24px) to avoid accidental taps on clinical touch monitors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined clinical tiering model: high-contrast pristine white surfaces framed by subtle cold-tinted borders and low-diffused, cool ambient shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: `#F8FAFC` base page background. Unbroken, matte, sterile.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shell Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` auth card base. Bound by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and layered with an ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.04), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Select Menus & Dropdowns)**: Elevated clinical popups with enhanced clarity:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.06), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.12)`. Border shifts to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Credential Verification Drawers)**: Heavy focus overlay with a high-opacity navy-tinted backdrop:
  `background: rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.60)` accompanied by `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Focus Glows**: No heavy skeuomorphic outer glows. Active inputs utilize a sharp dual-ring focus state: a 1px inner border of `#1E40AF` complemented by a 3px outer ring of `#1E40AF` at 15% opacity (`rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Balanced Geometric (Level 2)** shape language, projecting modern precision without appearing casual or juvenile.

### Shape Applications
- **Base Components (Inputs, Primary/Secondary Buttons)**: Radius of `0.5rem` (8px). Ensures crisp structural alignment on complex medical forms.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`)**: Radius of `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px–16px) for the central authentication card, creating a welcoming, polished enclosure.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`)**: Verification status chips, medical registration tags, society membership type markers, and count badges strictly use fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`).
- **OTP Verification Blocks**: `0.5rem` (8px) for individual numeric digit input cells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Institutional)**: Background `#0F294A`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1A365D`. Active state shifts to `#0A1C33`. Full-width layout on mobile auth cards, minimum height `48px` to ensure touch reliability.
- **Secondary / Action Button**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F294A`. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` with a border of `#94A3B8`.
- **Tertiary / Text Link**: Unstyled background, text `#1E40AF`, font weight 600. Underline on hover only.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Layout**: Label sits outside above the input using `label-md` in `#0F172A`, paired with an optional right-aligned helper link.
- **Input Field**: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **States**:
  - *Focus*: Border `#1E40AF`, outer shadow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.15)`.
  - *Error*: Border `#DC2626`, outer shadow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.15)`. Accompanied by a 12px error message with a crimson alert icon.
  - *Filled / Validated*: Border `#059669` with an inline emerald checkmark icon.

### 6-Digit OTP Verification Cells
- Six discrete input blocks arranged horizontally with `0.5rem` (8px) gaps.
- Dimensions: `48px` wide by `56px` high (desktop), `42px` by `50px` (mobile).
- Centered typography using `code-otp` (`24px`, bold).
- Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` resting state, transitioning to `2px solid #1E40AF` on active focus.

### Status Chips & Pills
- Small, compact badges with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Verified Member**: Emerald tinted background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#047857`), border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
- **AIOS Fellow / Gold Tier**: Warm saffron background (`#FFFBEB`), saffron text (`#B45309`), border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Pending Review**: Neutral slate background (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#475569`), border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Medical Header & Institutional Brand Card
- **Header Bar**: Fixed top strip featuring the formal association seal/crest on the left, an official divider line (`#E2E8F0`), followed by the subtitle "Medical Professional Portal".
- **Security Badge Banner**: A small clinical sub-banner integrated inside the card footer: `#F8FAFC` background, subtle lock glyph, and text: *"Authorized Medical Registry • Encrypted AIOS Authentication"*.
